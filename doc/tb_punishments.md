# Player Punishments [ IN-GAME ]
**Must-Know**:
- Severe cases don't require **any** warning, it doesn't matter if it's from the ban category or the mute category.
- Mild cases **require** warning players for the first offense.
- If a player repeats the punishment past the offenses given in the information below, the length of the offense will **double** on each new offense.

## Ban punishments
### Severe cases (Require clear evidence unless blatant)
1. **Cheating** – Using third-party software or hardware tools to gain an unfair advantage over other players. This includes everything from *Hack Information*.
   - **Punishment:** Immediate permanent ban 

1.2 🆕 **Cheating Association** - Playing **on purpose** with a cheater **without reporting it** and to explicitly **gain advantages** (like getting CW wins, winning boss battle matches easily, etc.).

- This requires **clear evidence**: video footage, screenshots, chat logs, direct confessions, etc. **Always provide multiple evidence** if the evidence is only circumstantial. **If the evidence is direct**, like explicit video footage or direct confessions, you don't need multiple evidence.
- **Punishments**: With clear evidence as above, **permanent, immediate ban** on the main account. **New accounts** in this case can be created and it's not considered ban-bypassing.
- **If you're unsure**, always discuss with staff and eventually vote (given enough evidence!)

2. **Severe Exploits** - Any exploit that is in the following list: Crashing Rooms, Crashing the servers, obtaining free MP/RT/Coins/items through exploits, or any exploit that can harm the game like packet modification. **If you're unsure whether something is a severe exploit, ask a developer**. 
   - **Punishment:** Immediate permanent ban 

3. **Game File Modifications** - Specifically, we disallow modifying the `cgd.dip` archive and `map.dat`, and in some special cases `model.dat` or `fx.dat` (see below)
   - **Punishment:**  
        - For `cgd.dip` and `map.dat` modifications (with evidence): **Immediate Permanent Ban**
        - For `model.dat`: **only if** the modification leads to advantages then it is a **permanent ban**.
        - For `fx.dat`: **only if** the modification is related to `fx.dat` -> `camera`, which leads to advantages, then it is a **permanent ban**.

4. **Coordinated attacks on the server/game** - DDoS attack, mass trolling, crashing the servers through exploits, crashing rooms through exploits.
   - **Punishment:** Immediate permanent ban 

5. **Distributing malware or cheats** - Deliberately spreading harmful software (viruses, spyware, ransomware) or spreading hack links, tutorials on how to hack the game, etc.
   - **Punishment:** Immediate permanent ban 

6. **Severe, repeated violations of any rule in this document, despite warnings and temporary bans**: When a user persistently breaks server rules, even after repeated warnings, mutes, or temporary bans—demonstrating a deliberate disregard for community guidelines and moderation efforts.
   - **Punishment:** Permanent ban 

7. **Evading bans using new accounts** - Ban evasion occurs when a user attempts to bypass an existing ban by creating or using new accounts, alternate devices, or technical workarounds (e.g., VPNs, IP spoofing).
   - **Punishments:** 
        - If the main account was banned **for cheating or doxxing** => Immediate permanent ban on the alt account (with clear evidence showing it's an alt)
        - If the main account was banned **temporarily** => Ban with the same suspension duration left of the main account.
        - *Note*: Other cases are ignored. For example, if someone is banned on a main account for cheating **association** (which is permanent but isn't cheating), then they can create another account.

## Kick Punishments
**To Know**: Before any kick, you must **warn** as the first punishment. Don't directly kick someone if you haven't warned them first.
 
The staff team can choose when to kick a player from a room, but **evidence** must be provided, and a **strong reason** must be provided.

**Good example:** Someone keeps abusing glitches or bugs in a public room that give them advantages, **and they evade room votekicks by leaving the room**

**Bad example**: Kicking someone because we dislike them, or because other players ask us to do it (in that case, they have a votekick system!)

## Mute Punishments / Chat Related
### Severe cases (Require clear evidence unless blatant)
8. **Personal data leakage/Doxxing** - Sharing private, sensitive, or identifying information about an individual without their consent. This includes (but is not limited to): full legal names (**if not already publicly disclosed by the target**), home/work addresses, phone numbers, email addresses(non-public), government id's, private photos/videos(non-consensual sharing).
    - **Punishments:** Immediate permanent ban

🆕 **Note**: First names are no longer considered doxxing.

🆕 **Note 2**: If you're unsure whether something is doxxing, open a staff vote.

9. **Severe cases of toxicity, racism (slang NOT included), lgbtiqfobia (slang NOT included), sexism (slang NOT included), insults - Hate Speech** 
   - **Punishments:** Immediate 7 days mute. Future offenses will be double the amount. **Always check previous offenses to know the correct length of the mute**.

10. **Threat of violence and severe bullying towards a player or their family** - Any explicit or implied threat to inflict physical/psychological harm on a player or their family, or systematic bullying designed to terrorize, humiliate, or isolate the victim.
       - **Punishments:** Immediate 7 days mute. Future offenses will be double the amount. **Always check previous offenses to know the correct length of the mute**.

11. **Explicit content, including nudity, including explicit content links**  - Refers to NSFW content being posted on the chat in this case it will be links since other stuff such as photos can't be posted.
    - **Punishments:** Immediate 7 days mute. Future offenses will be double the amount. **Always check previous offenses to know the correct length of the mute**.
 
12. **Evading mutes with new accounts** - Mute evasion occurs when a user attempts to bypass the mute punishment with a new account.
     - **Punishments:** Immediate Permanent Mute (in-game, use 9999 as the length). **Requires evidence that it's an alt account**

## Farming cases:
**Farming Experience, Kills, Missions, etc**
   - **Punishments** (Check previous punishments for the player first):
        - 1st offense: Warn + **Add the report in Admin Panel ("Farming Cases") + Discord**
        - Other offenses: **Add the report in Admin Panel ("Farmin Cases") + Discord with evidence** => The admin panel will take care of the correct punishment automatically.
    
The admin panel will automatically punish the player in the following way, **you only need to warn on first offense manually in-game**:

- 1st offense: Warn
- 2nd offense: 7 Day Ban & level, exp, mp, rt + stat reset.
- 3rd offense: 30 Day Ban + permanent ban alt accounts + level, exp, mp, rt & stat reset. + Inventory reset.

## Votekick cases:
**Votekick abuse** - **Stalking a person in multiple rooms** for the sole purpose of vote kicking them. To prove this, the target should submit 1 video proving the same person votekicks them in different rooms in a short period of time.
- **Punishments** (Check previous punishments for the player first)
    - Disable votekick for 1-14 days depending on the severity of the case.
    - Next punishments can be doubled. 

## Usage of glitches and bugs:
**Any spot outside the map or inside any object with no entrance, and any spot not meant to be reached in normal ways, OR bugs like Melee Swing Bug, Grenade Bug at round end, etc**

- Normal rooms punishments:
    - No punishment from staff members, use the vote kick system.
- Clan war rooms punishments:
    - Immediate 1 day ban with clear evidence. **Always check previous punishments for this player. Double it if they did it before**

## Report cases:
**Fake / Troll / Intentionally Misleading Reports in-game**

- **Punishments** (Always check previous punishments for the player, double the punishment if there's previous ones)
    - 1st Offense: Warning
    - Final Offense: 1 Day Ban

## Account Sharing
We assume that a player does not share their account. **Which means:**
- We don't enforce this rule - no account is banned if we found it's shared, **but**
- If the account is affected by any issue (like found cheating), **we will apply any rule with the assumption that it is not shared**.

## Additional Notes:
Always update the `#punishment` channel after you punish a player. Provide evidence, nickname, reason, and what punishment was given.
